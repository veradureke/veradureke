## About Me

I'm **Vera Dureke** (she/they), a Computer Science PhD student at the **University of Illinois Chicago**, researching algorithmic fairness and trustworthy AI with Prof. Abolfazl Asudeh. I'm supported by a **GEM Fellowship** and focused on how ML systems encode and amplify bias, and how to build systems people can actually trust.

My current research centers on **trustworthy (agentic) LLM systems**: compact information widgets attached to LLM and agent outputs that help users judge whether to trust what they're seeing.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an AI/VR internship at **NASA Glenn Research Center's GVIS Lab**, where I built an interactive VR exhibit with a conversational AI astronaut guide and developed an exoplanet detection ML system using Kepler light curve data.

I hold a double major in **Data Science and Mathematics** from Indiana University East (2026) and work as a Business Intelligence Analyst at **Presidio**. Outside research, I serve as President of **Blacks in Technology Chicago**.

## Highlighted Projects

### [Exoplanet Detection](https://github.com/veradureke/Exoplanet-Detection)
ML system for detecting planetary transits in Kepler stellar light curves. Random Forest + 1D CNN pipeline with SMOTE oversampling and precision-recall threshold tuning, integrated into a NASA Gradio app. Planet recall improved from 0% to 100% after addressing class imbalance.

### LUNA: AI Astronaut Guide (NASA Glenn)
Meta Quest 3 VR experience for an Artemis lunar exhibit featuring a conversational AI astronaut guide. Built in Unity 6 with a FastAPI server, Whisper speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and a RAG pipeline over NASA documents. Took it from early research through full deployment.

### [Step Necessity](https://github.com/dylanjayabahu/step-necessity)
Research collaboration on token-efficient reasoning via saturation-budget GRPO training on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B.

### [Technology Product Categorization](https://github.com/veradureke/Technology-Product-Categorization)
Multiclass classification of technology products using XGBoost, with tokenization of product descriptions and precision-focused feature engineering.

### [Deforestation Exploration](https://github.com/veradureke/Deforestation-Exploration)
Exploratory analysis of global deforestation patterns with geospatial analysis, visualization dashboards, and insights for environmental sustainability.

### [Seven Sages Brewing](https://github.com/veradureke/Seven-Sages-Brewing)
Predictive analytics for a craft brewing company: demand forecasting, time-series analysis, and market trend visualization.

## Research Interests

Algorithmic fairness, data ethics, trustworthy and agentic LLM systems, ML bias auditing, and responsible AI. I'm especially interested in how fairness constraints interact with model performance across demographic groups, how transparency tools like nutritional labels and datasheets can help people judge AI outputs, and the policy implications of biased automated systems.
